# Monthly report outline

1. **What was done:** pages published by type, pages reworked, consolidated, technical fixes.
2. **Indexing:** indexed versus not indexed per section, compared with last month.
3. **Search performance by page type:** impressions, clicks, average position. Label partial
   months as partial.
4. **Leads:** leads by page type and landing page (from the Clarity and CRM sheet), with
   company context where shared.
5. **AI visibility:** Bing AI citations and any tracked assistant citations.
6. **Effort, result, impact:** one table linking what was done to what changed and what it
   meant for the business.
7. **Wins and watch areas:** short, honest, with context for any dip.
8. **Next month:** the next batches, fixes and decisions needed from the business.
